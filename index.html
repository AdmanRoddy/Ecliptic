Two spots twenty feet apart are not the same yard.

A bed on the south wall and a bed on the north wall of the same house can differ by six hours of direct sun in December. Hardiness zone alone can't tell you that — it's the same number for both.

Sun position is calculated astronomically from your latitude, longitude, date, and time. Shadow accuracy depends on the heights and positions you enter for buildings and trees, and the model assumes level ground. Results are a planning aid, not a survey.
